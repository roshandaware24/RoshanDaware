01Who we are

This website is the personal portfolio of Roshan Daware, a digital marketing enthusiast and web developer based in Maharashtra, India. It presents my education, certificates, skills and projects. This policy explains what information the site handles, why, and what choices you have.

02Information you share with me

If you use the contact form or email me directly, I receive the details you choose to send: your name, your email address, any phone number you include, and the content of your message. Please share only what is needed for your enquiry.

03Information collected automatically

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard technical details, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested and the time of the request, to run and secure its service. This site does not currently use analytics tools. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

04How I use your information

I use the information you share only to:

  • Reply to your message and follow up on a project or job enquiry
  • Keep the website working and secure
  • Meet legal obligations where they apply

I do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list without your permission.

05Third-party services

This site relies on a few third-party services. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website
  • Google Fonts, which loads the typefaces
  • cdnjs, which delivers the Font Awesome icons
  • The form service that delivers contact messages to my inbox
  • LinkedIn, Instagram and GitHub, when you click their links

Please review those policies if you want more detail.

06Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. Third-party services may store technical data in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

07How long I keep messages

I keep contact messages only as long as needed to reply and continue the conversation. I delete them within 12 months of our last exchange, unless a working relationship, such as an ongoing project, needs them for longer.

08Sharing

I do not sell, rent or trade your information. I share it only with the service providers needed to run this site and respond to you, or when the law requires it.

09Security

I take reasonable steps to protect information, including serving this site over HTTPS. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send passwords, bank details or identity document numbers through the website or by email.

10Your rights

You can ask me what personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent for future contact. Applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 where it applies, may give you further rights. To make a request, email roshandaware2005@gmail.com. I will respond within a reasonable time.

11Children

This site is intended for general visitors and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent me information, email me and I will delete it.

12External links

This website links to LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own, and I am not responsible for them.

13Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
